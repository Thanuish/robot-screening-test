Phone controls
Hold MOVE and move the phone: the gripper moves the same way.
Let go of MOVE to move your hand without moving the robot.
Turn the phone left / right: the gripper turns. In task 3, tilting and twisting the phone also tilts and twists the gripper.
GRAB closes the gripper, RELEASE opens it.
RE-CENTER: point the phone at this screen and tap it if directions feel wrong.
View (keyboard): arrow keys turn and tilt, + / − zoom, R resets. Or drag and scroll with the mouse. Forward on the phone is always into the screen.
